About this tool

FTW global field boundaries sometimes lump two fields with different crop rotations into one polygon. This tool splits them using 8 years of USDA CDL history.

Split lines snap to the PLSS cardinal grid. The signal is rotation divergence — years where the two zones grew different crops.
