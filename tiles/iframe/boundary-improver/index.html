About this tool

These are suggestions for human review, not verified boundaries. About ~2/3 of high-signal split suggestions are correct — the rotation signal tells us the split is likely, not certain.

Fields of The World (FTW) global field boundaries sometimes lump two fields with different crop rotations into one polygon. This tool splits them using 8 years of USDA CDL history.

Split lines snap to the PLSS cardinal grid. The signal is rotation divergence — years where the two zones grew different crops.
